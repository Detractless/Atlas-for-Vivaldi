# 006 - Social media filter: status, 2026-11-01

What works, checked how, and what is still unproven. Written after the build, an independent code review, and a
second live review run by the lead (not the workers who wrote the tables).

## Summary

- Settings (Focus > Content shield > Social media filter) now does what it says: **Block social sites entirely**
  on all 8 sites, and **Block selected parts** on all 8 (YouTube, Reddit, Twitch, X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Snapchat).
- 46 (part, site) pairs are promised by Settings; **38 were seen working on the real site** in a signed-out browser, **8 are source-only** (their selectors come from maintained projects but the markup is only visible signed in, or behind a bot check).
- Tests: 679 JS unit tests pass; the one Python failure (`test_step6_writes_what_the_original_installer_wrote`) was there before this work.
- End to end (`node scripts/social_e2e_check.mjs`), in a throw-away Vivaldi with Atlas installed: scripts registered for all 8 sites; filter off adds nothing; a chosen part is hidden within about 0.1 s of the page starting (107 ms measured) and comes back when switched off without a reload; "block sites entirely" lands on the block page; other sites are untouched.
- Live review of every table (`node scripts/social_live_check.mjs <site>`): no guard trips, and with every part of a page on at once the page's landmarks and search box stayed visible on every page checked.

## To take effect

Atlas registers its page scripts when Vivaldi starts: **restart Vivaldi once**. A running Vivaldi keeps the old scripts.
Not done: restarting your Vivaldi (that needs your say-so).

## What is not proven, and how to find out

- **Signed-in markup.** X, Instagram and Facebook feeds are only visible signed in. After a restart, browse normally for a day, then read `atlas_social_health` from the Vivaldi profile (`Local App Settings/<extension id>`, key `atlas_social_health`): a part whose `everMatched` is false on a site you used is a stale selector; `trips` > 0 means the safety guard switched a part off there.
- **Reddit** answered the review browser with a bot check on its home and post pages (not bypassed). Re-run `node scripts/social_live_check.mjs reddit` later, with no other Reddit traffic for 25+ minutes.
- **Selector rot.** Sites change their markup: re-run the live review from time to time (`scripts/social_live_check.mjs <site>`), and refresh the offline fixtures with `scripts/social_fixtures.mjs <site>`.
- **Known limits.** The first style goes on about 0.1-0.3 s into a page load, so a hidden part may show for a moment on a first load. `old.reddit.com` is not covered. Instagram's follow buttons are found by the word "Follow" in about a dozen languages. YouTube's autoplay switch is YouTube's own and is given back when the part is switched off.
